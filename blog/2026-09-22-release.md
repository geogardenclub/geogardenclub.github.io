## In this release:

* Tasks can now be associated with gardens, beds, crops, varieties, and plantings.
* Active Tag chips (tapping a chip takes you to all the Observations with that tag).
* User, crop, variety, and forum images are not lost on update.
* Home/Gardens FAB no longer covers retail value toggle.


To get the app, see the [Getting Started page](/docs/user-guide/get-started/overview).
