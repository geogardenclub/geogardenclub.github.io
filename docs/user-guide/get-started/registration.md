# Registration

:::tip Step 2: Register an account with GeoGardenClub.
:::

When the GGC app comes up, it will show the Sign In screen if you have not logged in previously.  If this is your very first time using the GGC app, you will not be able to sign in since you have not registered yet.  

So, if you have not done so previously, tap on "Register" link to go to the Registration screen:

<img width="300" src="/img/user-guide/register-screen.png"/>

In the Register Screen, enter your email address, password, and password confirmation. (Your email address will be your UserID.)  Tap "Register" to create your account. Now the screen will look like this:

<img width="300" src="/img/user-guide/verify-email.jpg"/>


You must now find the verification email, which looks like this:

<img width="600" src="/img/user-guide/verify-email.png"/>

:::warning I didn't get the verification email! 
If you don't see the verification email in your inbox within a minute, then please check your Spam folder. In rare cases, an email provider will mark it as Spam.

Another possibility is that you made a typo when you entered your email address. You can test that by going back to the registration page and re-registering, checking carefully to make sure that you typed your email address correctly.

If neither of these hints result in a verification email, send us an email at info@geogardenclub.com and we will help you resolve the problem.
:::

Clicking on the link in the verification email will take you to a page that looks like this:

<img width="600" src="/img/user-guide/complete-verification-page-1.png"/>

Tap the button labeled "COMPLETE VERIFICATION". This should send you to the following page:

<img width="600" src="/img/user-guide/complete-verification-page-2.png"/>

Once you reach this page, you can go back to the app. In most cases, the app will automatically log you in and take you to the Create User Profile screen within a few seconds. If that doesn't happen, you can tap "Go back" link to return to the login page and sign in manually.

At this point, you're ready to create your profile, as discussed next.
