2027 Benchmarks and Projections

The previous page listed potential business and development activities intended to increase the viability of the business in 2027.

In this page, we present "benchmark" data from 2026 along with "projections" for 2027. These projections represent our estimate of the impact of implementing an appropriate subset of these activities on the business.

Customer Acquisition​

The Customer Acquisition Phase corresponds to the marketing activities that result in new downloads of the app.

In 2026, we spent $3,365 in marketing to acquire 307 downloads (as of September 30). In 2027, we plan to continue a similar level of marketing investment, split between digital and in person activities. Digital marketing expenses will focus on influencer content and Apple Ads which had our best ROI in 2026. We believe this will continue result in an increase in downloads.

Customer Evaluation​

The Customer Evaluation phase starts with the download of the app and ends with the first payment on a subscription plan.

Calculating Download -> First Payment

Calculating DL->1stPay for 2026 complicated due to the free three-month trial offer. This means that a user who subscribes at the end of Q2 will not make their first payment until Q3. This makes it difficult to calculate DL->1stPay within a single quarter for 2026. The "2026 Total" value (TBD%) is the most meaningful for DL->1stPay.

Most of our 2026 subscription drop-off happened early: in the first month after someone first pays, and at their first renewal. Subscribers who last about 6 months mostly stay. Churn also rises in fall and winter (Oct, Jan–Feb) and falls to zero in summer, which fits the seasonality of gardening.

In 2026, we experimented with adding a Free Preview Period in early April. This did not lead to an increase in users who made it to first subscription, and may actually have lowered the number of users who subscribed. In 2027, we will be removing the Free Preview Period to see if that increases the number of users that opt in for a subscription.

In 2026, we also reduced the free trial period from 6 months to 3 months. In 2027, we will further reduce the free trial period to 30 days, so that users see their first payment earlier in the growing season.
