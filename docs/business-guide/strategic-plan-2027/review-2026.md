# 2026 Successes and Failures

To begin, let's summarize the successes and failures of GeoGardenClub during 2026.

Our successes include:
* New features.  Insights, badge redesign
* Influencer marketing
* Launch of internship program
* New relationships: Hui Mala 'Ai, Grow Good Hawaii, Urban Garden Project.


That said, the fundamental failure of 2026 is that we did not get GeoGardenClub to a place where it is a viable/profitable business.

Put another way: 2026 did not show that GGC can obtain a consistent revenue stream that significantly exceeds the costs of (a) paid employees (b) marketing and (c) infrastructure. We estimate that the revenue stream required to demonstrate that GGC is a viable business is between 5K and 10K paying customers per month (i.e. $250K to $500K gross annual revenue).

Some significant "warning signs" from 2026 that GGC might not be a viable business include:
* Lack of success with online marketing attempts. (Instagram, Facebook, and Apple Ads failed to attract significant numbers of new customers.)
* Lack of organic growth. (i.e. existing users successfully getting their friends to use the system)
* Lack of "social proof". (only one additional App Store review and one Google Play review, no awards by publications)
* Lack of users. (Only a handful of active users (and revenue-producing) subscriptions as of October 2026).

Despite these warning signs, we remain optimistic that with appropriate improvements to our technology and marketing, we stand a good chance of growing GeoGardenClub into a viable business. The remainder of this strategic planning document is intended to help us figure out how to steer the business in that direction.
